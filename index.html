HELP CENTRE

How can we help?

Quick answers for making your practice time work better.

⌨

Keyboard controls

Every important exam action can be performed without a mouse.

  • TabMove to the next interactive control.
  • Shift + TabMove to the previous control.
  • EnterActivate the focused button, link, or selected control.
  • SpaceSelect an answer or activate a focused button.
  • ↑ ↓Move between radio-answer choices when the browser exposes them as a group.
  • EscClose the active dialog or leave the exam safely.
  • Alt + RRead the current exam question aloud.
  • Home / EndMove to the beginning or end of a page when supported by the browser.
◉

Screen reader support

Questions, options, timer, progress, selected states, review states, errors, and submission messages have semantic labels or live announcements.

When focus moves with Tab, ClearPath can announce the focused control. Use your screen reader's normal reading commands alongside ClearPath's keyboard shortcuts.

?

Voice & accessibility help

Use Answer by voice to say “Option B”, “B”, or a matching answer. Questions can be read aloud automatically and on demand.
